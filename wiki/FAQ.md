# Frequently Asked Questions

This FAQ addresses common questions about Vocab. If you can't find the answer to your question here, please reach out to our support team at support@vocab-boost.com.

## General Questions

### What is Vocab?
Vocab is an intelligent vocabulary learning application that uses spaced repetition and neural processing to help you learn and retain new words effectively. It's designed for language learners, students, professionals, and anyone looking to expand their vocabulary.

### Is Vocab free to use?
Vocab offers both free and premium tiers:
- **Free tier:** Access to core features including daily words, basic quizzes, and limited spaced repetition.
- **Premium tier:** Unlocks advanced features like unlimited custom lists, enhanced neural processing, offline mode, and ad-free experience.

### Which platforms is Vocab available on?
Currently, Vocab is available on:
- Android devices (7.0 Nougat and above)
- Web browsers (via our Progressive Web App)
- iOS version is under development and coming soon

### Is an internet connection required?
- **Free tier:** Yes, an internet connection is required for most features
- **Premium tier:** Includes offline mode that allows you to download word packs for offline study

### How does Vocab's learning algorithm work?
Vocab uses a sophisticated spaced repetition system (SRS) enhanced with neural processing to optimize review timing. The algorithm analyzes your response patterns, retention rates, and learning history to schedule reviews at the optimal time for long-term memory formation. See our [Spaced Repetition System](/wiki/Spaced-Repetition-System) page for detailed information.

## Account & Data

### How do I create an account?
1. Download the app or visit our web app
2. Tap "Sign Up" on the welcome screen
3. Enter your email or continue with Google/Apple
4. Create a password (if using email)
5. Complete your profile (optional)

### Is my learning data backed up?
Yes, all your learning data is automatically synced to the cloud when you're connected to the internet. If you use the app offline (premium feature), your data will sync the next time you connect.

### Can I use Vocab on multiple devices?
Yes, with a Vocab account, you can seamlessly switch between devices. Your learning progress, custom lists, and settings will automatically sync across all your devices.

### How do I delete my account and data?
1. Go to Settings > Account > Privacy
2. Scroll to the bottom and select "Delete Account"
3. Confirm your decision
4. Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted

## Technical Questions

### Why is the app using so much storage?
The storage usage depends on your settings and usage patterns:
- Word data with examples and audio can use 50-100MB
- Premium offline packs can use 200-500MB depending on size
- App cache can grow to 100MB with regular use

You can clear cache in Settings > Storage > Clear Cache without losing your learning data.

### How do I update the app?
For the best experience and latest features:
- **Android:** Updates are delivered through Google Play Store
- **Web App:** Automatically updates when you reload the page
- **Manual APK users:** Download the latest version from our website

### The app is crashing, what should I do?
If you experience crashes:
1. Ensure your device OS is updated
2. Clear the app cache (Settings > Apps > Vocab > Storage > Clear Cache)
3. Restart your device
4. If problems persist, contact support with your device model and OS version

### How do I enable notifications?
To enable learning reminders:
1. Go to Settings > Notifications
2. Toggle "Daily Reminders" on
3. Set your preferred reminder time
4. Optionally enable "Streak Alerts" and "Weekly Reports"

### Does the app work without Google Play Services?
Yes, Vocab can function without Google Play Services, though some features may be limited:
- Firebase sync will use alternative methods
- Push notifications may not work
- In-app purchases require alternative payment processing

## Troubleshooting

### Words aren't syncing between devices
If your words aren't syncing:
1. Ensure both devices are connected to the internet
2. Check that you're logged into the same account
3. Force a sync by pulling down on the Today screen
4. Verify sync is enabled in Settings > Account > Sync

### Audio pronunciation isn't working
If you can't hear audio:
1. Check your device volume
2. Ensure you have a stable internet connection (audio files are streamed)
3. Go to Settings > Audio and check audio download settings
4. Try switching between different pronunciation sources

### My streak was reset incorrectly
If your streak was reset unexpectedly:
1. Go to Settings > Account > Learning Stats
2. Check your last activity date
3. If a genuine mistake occurred, tap "Restore Streak" (available once per month)
4. Enable "Streak Protection" to get a buffer day

### Quiz answers are being marked incorrectly
If quiz answers are incorrectly marked:
1. Check that your answer matches exactly (including punctuation for fill-in-the-blank)
2. Report specific words with incorrect answers via Settings > Support > Report Issue
3. Try updating the app to get the latest content fixes
