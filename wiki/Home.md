# Vocab App Wiki

Welcome to the official wiki for Vocab, an advanced vocabulary building application designed to enhance your lexical prowess through spaced repetition learning and interactive quizzes.

## About Vocab

Vocab is an open-source Android application that combines cognitive science principles with modern mobile technology to create an effective vocabulary learning experience. The app implements a spaced repetition system (SRS) that schedules word reviews at optimal intervals for long-term retention.

## Key Features

- **Daily Word**: Learn one new carefully selected word each day with comprehensive definitions, examples, and usage contexts.
- **Interactive Quizzes**: Test your knowledge with customizable quizzes based on your learning history.
- **Machine Learning Algorithm**: Our adaptive algorithm optimizes review schedules according to your performance.
- **Progress Tracking**: Monitor your vocabulary growth with detailed statistics and visualizations.
- **Cross-Device Sync**: Seamlessly synchronize your progress across multiple devices.
- **Offline Mode**: Full functionality available without an internet connection.
